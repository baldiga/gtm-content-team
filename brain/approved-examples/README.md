# Approved examples: the gold standard

Every piece here passed the owner's approval in the review app. Writers and designers read the relevant examples before writing (rule R-05 in ../lessons.md), alongside the lessons. Match their voice, length, structure, CTA and link handling. Do not copy their topics: each topic is used once (R-01).

This public package ships with no real approved examples. The library grows after you start reviewing:

```bash
python3 brain/brain.py pull && python3 brain/export_approved.py <week>
```

Write a `notes-<week>.json` first with topic, audience, siblings, lessons and "why it worked".

What later examples should share (adjust to your voice):

- One claim per post, said early, in first person with soft hedging where it is honest.
- Short paragraphs, a blank line between them, one emoji at most per idea, ".." at most once.
- LinkedIn under your character limit, with a CTA. No link in the body.
- Siblings keep the same message and CTA, cut to the platform's length.
