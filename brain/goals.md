# Goals and cadence (template)

Fill this in with your own numbers, platforms and windows. The team reads this file together with `lessons.md` before planning a week.

## Goal by YYYY-MM-DD

- Target new followers across LinkedIn + Instagram + X combined (record a baseline with Publisher and Analyst before the first post).
- Target site visits, counted via the UTM convention in `utm-convention.md`.
- Note the weekly pace that would get you there.

## Publishing rules

- LinkedIn (personal): set a window if you already post in the morning (for example, only after 12:00 in your timezone). Default a mid-day slot if you want one.
- Instagram and X: the team picks the time unless you set one here.
- Nothing is scheduled or published without the owner's approval in the review app.

## Meeting batching

- New Granola meetings are not processed one by one. Batch every two days, or every 4-5 meetings, whichever comes first.
- Run a batch only when at least 2 meetings pass the quality bar (score 70+ for a full pack). Otherwise they wait in the idea bank for the next batch.
- In a batch, merge messages across meetings, list central topics, and give each topic one piece per platform (R-01). Do not repeat a topic that already appears in the last 2 weeks of approved examples.

## Long-term cadence

- Target: a small number of quality pieces per day across platforms, all from meetings. Grow toward it only while approval rate and quality hold.
